Create GitHub issues in acme/recipes for these, they came out of today's planning meeting:

1. Cooks can add all the ingredients of a recipe to a shopping list from the recipe page.
2. The shopping list puts the same ingredient from several recipes on one line.
3. Cooks can tick off the items they already have at home.
4. Cooks can share a shopping list with someone else through a link.

They all belong to the "Shopping list" milestone. Why: in last month's survey, 6 out of 10 cooks said they copy the ingredients into a notes app by hand before they go shopping.
